#  Sales Analytics Dashboard

## 🔍 About the Project
An interactive Sales Analytics Dashboard built with **Microsoft Excel**  
to analyze sales performance, regional distribution, and customer insights.

## 📄 Dashboard Pages

### 📌 Report 1 – Sales & Profit Performance
- **KPI Cards:** Sum of Sales, Sum of Profit (283,240), Average Discount,
  Total Quantity, Profit Margin
- **Category by Sales and Profit** – Bar Chart
- **Sum of Sales and Profit by Segment** – 100% Stacked Bar Chart
  *(Consumer | Corporate | Home Office)*
- **Sum of Profit and Sales by Month** – Bar Chart
- **Sum of Discount and Profit by Category** – Area Chart
- **Sum of Profit and Sales by Years** – Bar Chart (2011–2014)

### 📌 Report 2 – Regional Sales Performance & Shipping Analysis
- **Sum of Profit by Country** – Map Visual (Europe)
- **Average Shipping Duration** – KPI Card (3.97 days)
- **Sum of Sales by Ship Mode** – Pie Chart
  *(Economy: 60% | Economy Plus: 21% | Priority: 13% | Immediate: 6%)*
- **Sum of Profit by Country** – Filled Map

### 📌 Report 3 – Top Performers: Products & Key Customers
- **Top 10 Products by Sales** – Bar Chart
- **Bottom 10 Products by Sales** – Bar Chart
- **Top 10 Customers by Sales** – Bar Chart
- **KPI Cards:** Distinct Count of Customers (4,117) | Count of Customers (792)

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters
- Category Filter (Office Supplies, Furniture, Technology)
- Segment Filter (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office)
- Order Date Filter (2011–2014)
- Region Filter (Central, North, South)
- Ship Mode Filter
- Country Filter

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Slicers & Timelines
- Map Visuals
